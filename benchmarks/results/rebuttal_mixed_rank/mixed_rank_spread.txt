Interpretation:
- 'pad tax vs r_min' is the cost a LOW-rank tenant pays for sharing a
  batch with high-rank tenants: the mixed batch against a batch where
  every tenant runs at the fleet's lowest rank and nothing is padded.
  This is the quantity the uniform-rank limitation is about.
- 'vs uniform r_max' isolates whether a mixed batch costs anything
  BEYOND its padded shape. Zero-padding makes the batch tensors
  bit-identically shaped to a uniform r_max batch, so this should sit
  at ~0; a positive value is gather cost, not arithmetic.
- 'store vs padded' is the native-rank layout's footprint relative to
  pre-padding every adapter to r_max. Below 1.0x means padding can be
  confined to the transient batch tensor, so a mixed fleet's tenant
  ceiling is set by MEAN rank, not max.
- 'batches @ r_max' is the share of timed batches whose maximum native
  rank equals the fleet maximum. Near 100% means padding to the batch
  max is, in practice, padding to the fleet max — the pessimistic
  reading of these numbers is the realistic one, and rank-bucketed
  routing is the only thing that would change it.
- Latency flat down each N column is Finding 1 surviving a mixed-rank
  fleet: pool size enters no tensor shape, rank enters the batch
  tensors only, and the two axes stay independent.
