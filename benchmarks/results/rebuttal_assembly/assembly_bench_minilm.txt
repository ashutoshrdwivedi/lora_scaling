Interpretation:
- 'speedup' is baseline/indexsel end-to-end (ratio of seed means).
  >1 means the index_select gather reduced total latency.
- 'idx+comp' vs 'indexsel' isolates torch.compile's MARGINAL
  effect on the already-vectorized gather (the id->row Python
  lookup is eager in both, so this is the pure fusion upside).
- Assembly share collapsing toward 0% means the CPU-side
  bottleneck is gone; the forward then bounds latency.
- 'Result scatter' is the per-tenant slice + softmax that
  unbatches logits; it is variant-independent
  (same op regardless of assembler), timed on-device. Host-side
  transfer/serialization to callers is serving-layer, excluded
  here to match the forward's boundary.
